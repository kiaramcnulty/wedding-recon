# Type rules — hair & makeup artists (`entries` blocks label: HAIR & MAKEUP ARTIST)

These are wedding-day HAIR and/or MAKEUP artists, teams, and salons. **One joint type on
purpose:** most do both, and an artist who does only hair or only makeup belongs here too.
Content is about the getting-ready morning — the look, the timeline, the travel, the money.

## Wrong type? Two tiers (draft-contract has the full rule; write NO rows for either)
- `NOTBEAUTY!:<slug>` (STRONG — positive evidence, auto-removed) — the dossier POSITIVELY
  shows a different business: a nail salon, barbershop, med spa / injectables / laser
  clinic, tanning or waxing studio, lash-extension-only or brow-only bar, permanent-makeup
  / microblading studio, massage or skincare-only spa, beauty-supply retailer, cosmetology
  school, wig / hair-restoration shop — or another vendor type entirely (a bridal DRESS
  shop, photographer, planner). (Signals: the whole site is nails/lashes/injectables/gowns;
  a service menu with no hair or makeup service on it at all.)
- `NOTBEAUTY:<slug>` (SOFT — looks off but not certain) — draft the row(s) normally and
  flag; the orchestrator vets before removing. **An everyday hair salon with no visible
  bridal work is THIN, not NOT* — a salon that does weddings often just doesn't say so on
  the page we crawled.**
- KEEP (never a flag): an artist who does **only hair** or **only makeup**; an artist who
  serves **the bride only** and not the party; a salon or spa with a genuine bridal
  hair-and-makeup service alongside its everyday menu; a team that also books non-wedding
  events. None of these are wrong-type — say what they do and move on.

## service_region — REQUIRED on every row
- Where they'll travel, sourced: stated travel radius ("Denver metro + 60 miles"), site
  copy, on-location policy, instagram bio. **Most of these vendors are mobile** — the
  radius IS the answer, so prefer it over a street address. A studio-based artist's
  city/metro is an acceptable sourced fallback ("Colorado Springs area"). Nothing at all →
  the run's state. If they clearly travel far ("destination", "will travel anywhere"),
  append " + destination". Never invent a narrow region; never blank (upload hard-fails).

## What couples actually want captured
- **Services + scope, up front.** Hair, makeup, or both. Bride only, or the whole bridal
  party (bridesmaids, mothers of the bride/groom, flower girls, sometimes groomsmen)? Team
  size, and whether they can cover a large party in one morning — a solo artist and an
  eight-artist team are different products.
- **Style, in real words.** Natural / soft glam / full glam / boho / editorial /
  classic-timeless; airbrush vs. traditional foundation. Note experience with a range of
  skin tones and hair textures (curly, coily, fine, extensions) when a source says so —
  couples search on exactly this and it's a genuine differentiator.
- **Travel policy — the shape, not just a number.** Do they come to you, or is the price
  only that low at their studio? Free within X miles / the metro, then a fee or per-mile
  charge? Is there a party-size or dollar minimum for on-location service, an early-start
  fee for a pre-dawn call time, or a parking/accommodation charge for far venues? This is
  the thing couples get blindsided by — carry it whenever the dossier has it.
- **Firsthand anecdotes are the highest-value content for this type** (more than any other
  vendor category — Kiara, 2026-07). What a real bride paid, whether the trial matched the
  wedding day, whether the look **held up through heat, tears, photos, and dancing**,
  whether the artist ran on time with a full party to get through, how they handled a
  nervous bride or a bridesmaid who hated her look. Attribute it ("a bride on reddit said
  …", "a review mentions …"). Carry the wart when a source has one (ran late, look was
  heavier than requested, trial cost not credited toward the day).
- **How booking works:** trial/preview expected or optional, how far ahead they book,
  deposit/retainer, contract minimums.

## price_details specifics
- **This type usually POSTS its rates — dig them out of the dossier rather than defaulting
  to "quote only."** The shapes to look for: bride hair, bride makeup, bride hair + makeup
  combined; per-person bridal-party rates (often priced separately for hair vs. makeup);
  trial / preview fee and whether it's credited toward the wedding; add-ons (lash strips or
  extensions, hair extensions installation, veil placement, touch-up kit, an artist staying
  on-site for touch-ups); travel fee / mileage / minimums / early-start fee; deposit terms.
- **Wedding pricing only.** Ignore prom, homecoming, and general special-event rate cards
  even when they're on the same page — they mislead couples about what a wedding costs.
- Never invent a number. "Quote only" is the honest fallback when nothing is posted, but on
  this type it should be the exception, not the default.
